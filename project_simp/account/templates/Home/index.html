Care & Questions

Why SIMP?

What makes SIMP shoes different?

Every SIMP shoe is crafted with premium materials and offers complete customisation – from colors to patterns – so you get a one‑of‑a‑kind pair.

How does customisation work?

Log in, head to the Design Studio, choose your base shoe, and play with colors, patterns, and materials. Preview in 3D and order directly!

Is shipping really free?

Absolutely! We offer free shipping on every order, no minimum purchase required.

What payment methods do you accept?

We support eSewa, Khalti, and soon more digital wallets. Cash on delivery is also available in major cities.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about custom shoes, ordering, and shipping.

Q1
How much does customization cost?
▾
Pricing depends on the style and customization level — starting around Rs. 2,500.
Q2
Will the print fade or peel over time?
▾
No — we use direct-to-shoe printing that bonds into the material, so it won't peel or crack with normal wear.
Q3
What file formats can I upload?
▾
PNG, JPG, and SVG files work best. For highest print quality, use a high-resolution image.
Q4
Which parts of the shoe can I customize?
▾
You can customize the upper panel, tongue, and heel — colors, patterns, and your own uploaded artwork.
