Our Mission

Bringing Custom Shoes to Every Foot in Nepal

SIMP was born from a simple idea: everyone deserves shoes that fit their style, their personality, and their feet. We combine direct-to-shoe printing technology with hand-finishing craftsmanship to create footwear that's truly yours.

From the streets of Kathmandu to the hills of Pokhara, we deliver custom-designed shoes with local payment options — eSewa, Khalti, and card — so anyone in Nepal can design and order their perfect pair.
